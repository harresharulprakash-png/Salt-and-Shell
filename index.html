Our Story

Born by the sea.

Salt & Shell began with a simple idea: bring the warmth of a Mediterranean coast kitchen to the south of Sri Lanka. Every dish starts with the morning's catch, hand-picked herbs, and slow, honest cooking.

We are a small family-run spot. The wood-fire grill, the open kitchen, the long communal table — it is all built to make you feel like you have stopped by an old friend's house for lunch.
